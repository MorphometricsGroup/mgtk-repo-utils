# Changelog

Entries from 0.1.1 on are generated by release-please from the Conventional
Commits of each release. A change that alters generated output (view layout,
wording) says so in its commit; a change that asks consumers to do something is
a breaking change (`!`), and only a large one gets a page under
`docs/migrations/`. The predecessor is repo-kit (`MorphometricsGroup/repo-kit`,
0.1.0 to 0.3.0); its history stays there, and moving a consumer from it is
[docs/migrations/from-repo-kit.md](docs/migrations/from-repo-kit.md).

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-11)

The first release: the tools of repo-kit 0.3.0 as one tool installed from this
repository's git URL and pinned by commit from a consumer's hooks (design
revisions 5 and 5.1), with `pin` in `check` and PyYAML as a declared
dependency. Known defect: the hook repository installed with prek but not with
pre-commit, because the root distribution depended on workspace members that
pip cannot resolve (fixed in 0.1.1).
